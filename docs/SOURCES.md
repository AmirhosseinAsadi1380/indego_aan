# Sources: third-party models, data and methods

What this project uses from others, what for, and why (with licences). Datasets and body models are downloaded by the
user and never redistributed here; only derived summary numbers are in `results/`.

| source | used for | why / notes |
|---|---|---|
| indego_sim (github.com/AmirhosseinAsadi1380/indego_sim) | everything | the human + Indego simulation all experiments run in |
| MyoSuite `myosuite/core/muscle_conditions.py` (CumulativeFatigue, MUSCLE_FATIGUE_PARAMS), Apache-2.0 | 3CC-r fatigue model and parameters in `indego_aan/deficits.py` | per-muscle-group F, R, r from Rakshit et al. 2021 (J Biomech 127:110695), "Default" from Frey-Law et al. 2012 with r of Looft et al. 2018; ported with attribution |
| DUO-GAIT (Zhou et al. 2023, Sci Data 10:543, doi 10.1038/s41597-023-02391-w; data: Zenodo 10.5281/zenodo.7415758; code: github.com/HPI-CH/fatigue-dual-task-data) | calibration target for the fatigue model | 16 healthy adults, 6-min walks before/after a sit-to-stand protocol with a vest of 30 % body mass to exhaustion. Fatigue effect on single-task walking: stride length 1.44 -> 1.41 m (-2 %, p = 0.02), speed n.s.; from the per-subject aggregates (`scripts/duo_gait.py`, `processed.zip` only): speed -0.9 %, stride time -0.9 %, stride length -1.9 %, stride-time CV +16 %. In the simulation this protocol corresponds to starting the walk with pre-fatigued hip and knee extensors (`Fatigue3CCr.reset(MF=...)`). The Zenodo record shows no licence field (the paper is CC BY 4.0, the code MIT), so the data stay local in `data/`. |
| Waterloo Indego dataset: Nasiri, Dinovitzer, Manohara & Arami, "Coordinated human-exoskeleton locomotion emerges from regulating virtual energy", PLOS ONE 2025 (doi 10.1371/journal.pone.0292334); data: Figshare 10.6084/m9.figshare.24910953, CC BY 4.0 | the person walking in the Indego: sim-vs-real check of gait, Indego sensors (encoders, thigh IMUs) and EMG timing (`indego_aan/waterloo.py`, `scripts/waterloo_indego.py`); open-loop replay of the controller (`scripts/paan_real_replay.py`) | the only public data of people in an Indego: 9 healthy adults on a treadmill at 0.60 / 0.75 / 0.85 m/s without the exo, in the Indego with zero applied torque, and with the VER controller; onboard exo log 200 Hz, mocap, GRF, 16-channel EMG. Kept outside the repository (4.3 GB; the scripts take its folder as an argument); derived numbers in `results/waterloo/`. Data quirks found and handled are listed in the module docstring (the logged torque is commanded, not measured; subject 9's file names; subjects 2 and 8 without mocap CSVs). |
| KINESIS (Simos et al. 2025, arXiv 2503.14637; github.com/amathislab/Kinesis), code BSD-3-Clause; pretrained policies on Hugging Face (`amathislab/kinesis-models`, BSD-3-Clause-Clear) | the learned, muscle-driven human (`scripts/kinesis/`, `indego_aan/kinesis_human.py`) | an 80-muscle motion-imitation policy (mixture of experts) for the same MyoLeg model as indego_sim; served to indego_sim at 30 Hz, fine-tuned with the exo folded into its own model. Needs the SMPL body model (smpl.is.tue.mpg.de) and KIT motions from AMASS (amass.is.tue.mpg.de), both registration-only and not redistributable. Training runs on Linux. |
| Stable-Baselines3 PPO (Raffin et al. 2021, JMLR 22:268), MIT | teacher training (`scripts/train_teacher.py`) | maintained, CPU-friendly, runs the simulation in parallel worker processes |
| Gait-tracking reward instead of an AMP discriminator | teacher reward `r_gait` (`indego_aan/env.py`) | with one deterministic simulated person a discriminator has little to learn from, so the teacher scores the human joint angles against the person's own fresh curve at the gait phase (a slower stride with a normal pattern is not penalised). Deviations within 2 fresh SDs are free: without that band the v1 teacher pushed a fresh person toward the mean curve. |
| Residual policy learning (Silver et al. 2018, arXiv 1812.06298; Johannink et al. 2019, ICRA) | teacher v3 (`AANEnv(base="paan")`) | the policy learns a correction on top of the personal assist-as-needed controller, after policies trained from scratch kept assisting fresh people (v1, v2) |
| CMA-ES (Hansen 2016, arXiv 1604.00772; the `cma` package) with common random numbers | tuned teacher (`scripts/tune_teacher_aan.py`) | four PPO runs (v1-v4, ~30 h) did not beat the hand-designed personal AAN: fall outcomes are mostly decided by the episode's deficit draw, which swamps the gradient. Tuning a few parameters of a privileged, deficit-scheduled AAN with every candidate scored on the same episodes removes that noise. |
| Margin of stability, extrapolated COM (Hof et al. 2005, J Biomech 38:1-8) | teacher reward `r_stab` | sagittal only, with the anterior floor set by the fresh gait (the XCoM passes the front toe by up to 0.2 m in normal single support) |
| MuscleMimic (github.com/amathislab/musclemimic, arXiv 2603.25544), Apache-2.0 | not used | a newer, permissively licensed alternative to KINESIS (JAX + MuJoCo Warp, released checkpoints); training needs Linux with an NVIDIA GPU |
